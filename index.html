About Us · Beginning

Since 2004, exporting fine engineered vanity tops.

Since 2004 we have been successfully exporting to the United States and Canada, distributing through Cultured Marble Manufacturers, Building & Plumbing Suppliers, General Contractors and Home Centers.

Our factory is located in Juárez, N.L., 160 miles south from our Distribution Center, conveniently located in Laredo, Texas. We specialize in fine quality Engineered Vanity Tops, designed specifically for homes, hotels and motels, assisted living retirement centers and multi-family dwellings.
